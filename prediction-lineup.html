How these predictions work

Reviewed lineups combine recent team selections with public previews and community consensus. Within the reviewed formation, compatible players with higher nailed estimates are prioritized in the starting XI. Automatic estimates use the same minutes-first selection as a deterministic fallback.

The nailed percentage is projected minutes ÷ 90, rounded to a whole number. It is a useful rotation-risk estimate, not a calibrated probability or an official team prediction.
